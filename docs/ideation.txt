1. **Core Concept & Problem Statement**: Describe "Second Opinion" as a health-tech tool designed to provide preliminary help to pharmasists. As it happens that many people in rural / semi-urban part of India directly go to pharmacies for their healthcare needs completely bypassing the a healthcare professional due to multiple reasons ranging from cost and availablility. With this tool we want to enable the pharmacists to provide a better service to these people by using the power of AI. 

2. **High-Level Functionality**: Currently we are focusing on the Android app, since the application is focus on issues in rural / semi-urban areas we will have to support local dilect as well. The app will provide a functionality for the pharmacist to record audio of the patient's symptoms. Since during recording there might be other people around as well who will be speaking, these people can be different customer of the pharmacy or family members of the patient, sometimes it happen there are multiple people at pharmacy as well who might be asking question or discussing the situation. So the deivce should have a on platform AI capabilities to hear the complete recording then separate different voices and then transcribe them into text and then assign weight to these text based on who was speaking. Then based on these weights we can discard the irrelevant text and only keep the text that is relevant to the patient's symptoms. Once we have the filtered weighted text we can will send the data to backend for further processing.
Tha backend will take this text input and use vairous LLM tools to again pre process the text and extract the relevant symptoms and other relevant information like age, gender, location, etc. Once we have the final text we will send it to a medical AI model to get the final diagnosis. The diagnosis will be sent back to the pharmacist along with a suggested prescription, which they can review and then have the option to either use it or not.
